Account Setup SOP
📄 SOP-SYS-FB-002 · Owner: Ben · v3

1. Purpose & Scope

This SOP covers the full process for setting up new Facebook outreach accounts for appointment booking campaigns. It applies to all team members responsible for account creation and initial configuration.

2. Roles & Responsibilities

  • Account Manager — Creates and configures the FB account
  • Team Lead — Reviews and approves account setup
  • Admin — Provides access credentials and permissions

3. Tools Required

  • Facebook Business Manager — business.facebook.com
  • Tracking spreadsheet — Google Sheets link
  • Internal CRM dashboard

4. Procedure

Step 1: Create Business Manager Account

Navigate to Business Manager and click "Create Account". Fill in the business name exactly as "Eminence & Co" and use the primary admin email.

Step 2: Configure Ad Account

Under Business Settings > Ad Accounts, click "Add" > "Create a new ad account". Set the timezone to GMT and currency to GBP.

Step 3: Assign Permissions

  • Add team members with Advertiser access
  • Add Team Lead with Admin access
  • Verify all pixel permissions are granted

5. Key Definitions

  • BM — Business Manager
  • Pixel — Facebook tracking code installed on landing pages
  • CAPI — Conversions API for server-side tracking
